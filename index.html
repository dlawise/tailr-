Tailor a CV to any role in 10 minutes
  1. Paste the advert in step 2 and read the fit check.
  2. Pick the 5 to 8 phrases the advert repeats. Those are its keywords.
  3. Use them where they are true: headline, summary, skills and two or three achievements.
  4. Lead each role with the achievements closest to the advert. Resume mode does this for you.
  5. Answer the gap questions in section 5 honestly. If you have nothing, leave it out and address it in the cover letter.
  6. Write the why this company sentence in the Apply kit.
  7. Download it, read it as plain text, then apply.
Write strong achievements

Formula: action verb + what you did + how + the result.

Weak: "Responsible for weekly reports."
Stronger: "Built a weekly Power BI report for 12 depot managers, cutting preparation time by 6 hours." Only use numbers that are true. If you do not know the exact figure, give an honest range or say "around".

No result yet? Say the scale: how many people, customers, records, sites or how often.

Summary formula

[Job title] with [years] of experience in [three strengths]. [One proof point with a number]. [Optional: what you want next]. Keep it to two or three sentences, about 25 to 70 words, and change the strengths to match each advert.

UK CV rules and ATS tips
  • Reverse chronological order, newest first. Usually two pages at most.
  • Leave out a photo, date of birth and marital status. Town or city is enough, no full address.
  • Plain headings: Profile, Experience, Education, Skills. Avoid tables, columns, text boxes and icons, which applicant tracking systems can misread.
  • Send a PDF unless the employer asks for Word. Name it like "Your Name - CV".
  • Use the advert's own wording where it is true, and keep date formats consistent.
  • Keep a master CV with everything, then tailor a copy per role.
Career change, gaps or little experience
  • Lead with a summary that explains your direction in one line.
  • Put transferable skills, projects, volunteering and courses where experience would go.
  • Explain a gap in one honest line, for example a career break or study. Years alone are fine instead of months.
  • Use the advert questions in section 5 to find evidence you may have overlooked.
Keep your CV current: questions to ask every three months
  • What did I finish, launch or improve?
  • What did I save: time, money or errors?
  • How many people, customers or records did I handle?
  • What new tools or methods did I learn?
  • Did I get praise, an award or extra responsibility?
  • Has my title, scope or team changed?

Add each answer with the achievement builder. Or log it as a win in the Career tab and use Add to CV.

Before you apply checklist
  • The advert is still open and you meet the must-haves.
  • The company name and role are correct in the cover letter.
  • Your contact details and links are right.
  • Numbers and dates match across your CV and cover letter.
  • The file is named properly and opens cleanly.
  • A follow-up date is set in Applications.
