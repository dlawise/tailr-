Apply faster: autofill bookmark

A bookmark that fills your details into an application page when you tap it. It cannot attach files, so you still upload your CV. Some sites block bookmarks like this, and forms inside embedded frames cannot be filled. The bookmark contains your personal details, so keep it private and make a new one after changing them.

Mac: show your bookmarks bar (View, Show Favorites Bar), then drag this link onto it: Tailor autofill

iPhone: tap Copy bookmark code. In Safari, open any page and tap Share, then Add Bookmark, name it Tailor autofill and save. Open Bookmarks, tap Edit, open that bookmark, replace its address with the pasted code, then tap Done. On an application page, open Bookmarks and tap it.
